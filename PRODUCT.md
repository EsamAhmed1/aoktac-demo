## Stack

Static HTML, CSS and one JavaScript module, no build step, matching the existing demo convention. Lenis loads from a CDN for smooth scrolling. Films are scrubbed by the page's own requestAnimationFrame loop (no GSAP). Three.js 0.169 from jsDelivr (import map) renders the 3D bottle and machine showcase, loaded only when that section is near. The scroll story is a Higgsfield-generated film scrubbed by scroll. Vercel static deploy only after Rob approves it.

## Users

Owners, plant managers and procurement leads at bottling, beverage, food, dairy, edible oil, home care and personal care plants. They are buying or replacing a PET blow molding machine or a whole bottle line. They compare suppliers on output per hour, container size range, cavity count, build quality and after-sales service. Many read English as a second language and browse on desktop at work or on a phone on the factory floor.

The demo's first audience is Aoktac's own management. It is a sales demo to win the website build.

## Product Purpose

The site presents Taizhou Aoktac Machinery Co., Ltd., a manufacturer of PET stretch blow molding machines in Linhai, Taizhou, Zhejiang, China. It shows how a machine turns a preform into a finished bottle, helps a buyer find the model that fits their container, and gets that buyer to contact sales. Success is a quote request or a WhatsApp or email contact with a specific container size and model in mind.

## Positioning

Aoktac publishes a full TEC BLOW range from a 1-cavity 30 L machine to a 12-cavity machine rated at 24,000 bottles per hour, with a complete spec sheet for each model. Krones and Sidel sell the same category with photo-led corporate sites and no process demonstration. Aoktac can show the process and let a buyer match their own bottle to a machine.

## Operating Context

- Two-step reheat stretch blow molding: preforms are heated in an infrared oven, stretched by a rod and blown in a mold.
- Product categories on the current site: One-Step Injection Stretch Blow Molding Machine, Two-Step Stretch Blow Molding Machine (High-Speed and Standard).
- Containers from 100 ml to 30 L in PET, PC, PP, PE and HDPE.
- Turnkey bottle production line and packaging solutions for food, beverage, milk and dairy, beauty care, oil and lubricants, and home care.
- Services: prototyping, laboratory tests, PET bottle design, blow mold design and manufacture, start-up and installation, maintenance audits, spare parts, upgrades, staff training.

## Product Principles

1. Show the process, do not describe it. The bottle being made is the proof.
2. Every number on the page is a published spec. Anything missing reads as "on request", never estimated.
3. Lead the buyer from their container to a named machine to a sales contact.
4. Plain English for second-language readers: short sentences, concrete nouns, units always shown.
